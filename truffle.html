TRUFFLEBAKES: A logo design for a pastry Brand

Trufflesbake is an artisan pastry brand dedicated to crafting exquisite, decadent treats with a touch of elegance. Its logo features a sophisticated monogram uniting the letters 'T' and 'B', seamlessly connected by a fluid stroke that artfully mimics the smooth, graceful swirl of cake icing.
